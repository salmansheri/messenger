# Rubric

A WhatsApp-style messaging service where verified users exchange text messages in direct and group conversations.

## Language

### People

**User**:
A person with a verified email address who can sign in and take part in conversations. Identified by email; has a display name.
_Avoid_: Account, member (unless speaking of a group), customer

**Verification code**:
A short-lived one-time code emailed to a User to confirm their email at signup or to reset a forgotten password. Never used as a second factor at login.
_Avoid_: OTP (in domain language), PIN, token

### Conversations

**Conversation**:
A shared message thread between two or more Users. Either direct or group.
_Avoid_: Chat, thread, room, channel

**Direct conversation**:
A Conversation between exactly two Users. At most one exists per pair of Users.
_Avoid_: 1:1 chat, DM, private chat

**Group**:
A Conversation among several Users with a name and administrators.
_Avoid_: Group chat, channel

**Participant**:
A User's membership in a Conversation, with a role (admin or member) and a join time.
_Avoid_: Member (outside a Group), attendee

**Admin**:
A Participant in a Group who may add or remove other Participants.
_Avoid_: Owner, moderator

### Messages

**Message**:
A piece of text sent by a Participant to a Conversation. Visible only to Participants who joined at or before it was sent.
_Avoid_: Chat, post, text

**Client message ID**:
An identifier generated by the sending client that makes a retried send count as the same Message.
_Avoid_: Idempotency key, request ID

**Sequence number**:
The position of a Message within its Conversation, assigned by the server. It defines the canonical order of Messages and is how clients resume after being offline.
_Avoid_: Timestamp order, message index

### Receipts

**Delivered position**:
The Sequence number of the latest Message a Participant's client has confirmed receiving. Only ever moves forward.
_Avoid_: Delivery cursor, ack

**Read position**:
The Sequence number of the latest Message a Participant has seen; never above their Delivered position. Only ever moves forward.
_Avoid_: Read cursor, last seen (which means presence)

**Unread count**:
How many Messages a Participant has not yet read: the latest Sequence number minus their Read position. Sending a Message moves the sender's Read position to it, so their own Messages never count; it also means replying marks everything before the reply as read.
_Avoid_: Badge, pending

**Message status**:
How far a Message has got, shown only to its sender: sent, delivered once every other Participant's Delivered position has reached it, read once every other Participant's Read position has.
_Avoid_: Receipt state, tick

### Presence

**Online**:
A User is online while at least one of their connections is open. Closing one of several connections does not change it; it ends a short grace period after the last one closes.
_Avoid_: Active, available, logged in

**Last seen**:
When an offline User's last connection ended. Shown only while they are offline.
_Avoid_: Last online, last active

**Typing indicator**:
A live notice that a Participant is typing in a Conversation. It is passed on to the other Participants and never stored.
_Avoid_: Typing status, composing

### Push

**Device**:
A phone, tablet or browser a User registered to receive push notifications. Identified to the push provider by its token; a token belongs to one User at a time.
_Avoid_: Client, endpoint, subscription

**Push notification**:
A short notice (who wrote, and the start of the Message) sent to a Device of a Participant who has no open connection when a Message arrives. Never sent to the sender or to someone already receiving the Message live.
_Avoid_: Alert, notification (alone, which is ambiguous with live events)
