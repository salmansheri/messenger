# Frontend: Stories

Read it before working in this area and keep it up to date. The backend is `docs/backend/stories.md`; the spec is #66.

- Tray and viewer (#74, `features/stories`): the generated client was regenerated against the real app (`./mvnw spring-boot:run`), which added `tray`/`trayOptions`/`trayQueryKey`, `view`, `viewers`, `post`, `delete_` and the types `StoryTray`, `Person` (one tray entry), `StoryResponse`, `StoryViewer`, `StoryQuote`. The operation names are the controller's (`post`, `delete_`, `view`): generic, so import them by name from `@/lib/api` and do not shadow them.
  - **Tray** (`StoryTray`, in `ConversationIndex` under the list's heading row): the `trayOptions()` query ordered by `orderTray` (pure, `tray.ts`): your own entry first (always, since #75: with a "+" while you have no live Story), then people with unseen Stories, the one who posted most recently first, then people whose Stories you have all seen, also newest first. With nobody else having a Story only your own entry is there (nothing at all before your profile has loaded). Each entry is a button named by `trayEntryLabel` ("Grace Hopper, unseen Stories", "Grace Hopper, Stories seen", "Your Story") with the person's `Avatar` inside a ring: `ring-accent` while unseen, `ring-rule` once seen; the first name under it is `aria-hidden` (the button carries the name).
  - **Live**: `parseEvent` reads `story.posted {story, poster}`, `story.removed {storyId, posterId}` and `story.viewed {storyId, viewerId, viewedAt, viewCount}`; `recordStoryEvent` (`sync.ts`) puts them into the tray through `applyStoryEvent` (pure: a posted Story joins its person's entry, or a new entry; your own from another tab joins your own; a removed Story leaves, and the person with their last one; a view raises your own Story's `viewCount`, never lowers it; a repeat or an unknown Story changes nothing) behind `patchTray`, with the same "a request under way is restarted instead of patched" rule as the list. `catchUp` asks for the tray again after a reconnect. `useProfileRefresh` also asks for the tray, so tray Avatars recover after their links run out.
  - **Viewer** (`StoryViewer`): the browser's own modal `<dialog>` dressed like `ImageViewer` (dark glass, a centred card, Close focused once it is open: `showModal` alone would focus the first button, Pause, and React's `autoFocus` does not survive it), driven by the pure state machine `viewer-machine.ts` (`openViewer`, `step`, `current`; Vitest). It opens at the chosen person's first unseen Story (their first when all are seen); each Story shows for `STORY_MS` (5s) and a photo's time starts only once it has loaded (`loaded`); after a person's last Story come the next person's (from their first unseen), going back from a person's first Story reaches the previous person's last, and after the last person it closes. The people are the tray's order when it opened; while it is open, tray changes are fed in (`sync`): the Story shown stays, a removed one moves on to what followed it, and nobody new is added. The right two-thirds and the left third of the card are buttons ("Next Story", "Previous Story"); ArrowRight/ArrowLeft do the same; a press longer than 250ms is a hold, which pauses while held and is not taken as a tap; Space (whatever has focus) and the Pause button (named 'Pause' or 'Play' by what it will do) toggle a pause that a hold does not undo. Time in a hidden tab does not count (`visibilityState`), so Stories do not play on, and are not reported as seen, while nobody is looking. Escape, Close or the end call `onClose` with the person last shown, and focus goes to their tray entry once the dialog is gone (the first entry if theirs has left). A `role=status` says `storySummary` ("Grace Hopper's Story, 2 of 3, posted 14:02") as each Story comes up; the visible header has the segmented progress bar (`aria-hidden`), the Avatar, the name and the time. Names under the tray's Avatars are in `font-serif`, like the list's. A photo's `alt` is `photoAlternative` ("Photo by Grace Hopper: caption"); a text Story is drawn on its tone (`TONES` from `person-avatar.tsx`, the Avatar tones' classes, never hard-coded colours). Each Story's content mounts with `viewer-in`, which the global reduced-motion rule in `globals.css` cancels, so reduced motion removes only that transition: the timer, the bar and Pause behave the same.
  - **Views**: when a Story of someone else's is shown (a photo once loaded) and is not already seen, `sdk.view` is called once per Story for the viewer's lifetime (the effect is keyed by the Story's id, because the objects around it are new on every tick; a failed report is not sent again while the viewer is open, and the Story stays unseen in the tray); success marks it seen in the tray (`recordStorySeen` → `markSeen`, pure), which turns the ring quiet once all are seen. Your own Stories are never reported.
  - **Photos** go through `useImageRecovery` with `useTrayRefresh` (the tray asked for again, a request under way reused): one silent refetch for a link that ran out, then "Couldn't load the photo." (a `role=status`) with Retry, after which focus goes to Close; the timer waits meanwhile (the photo has not loaded), so the viewer stays on it until the person moves on with the sides or the keys.
  - The UI says "Story", never "status".
  - **Not exercised in a browser** (the owner stopped using Playwright, so no e2e was written or run): the tray's look and order on screen, the rings, the viewer's layout on a phone and a desktop, the timer and the bar in real time, tapping, holding and the keys, focus going back to the tray entry, the live tray on a second screen, the view reports and the seen ring, photo recovery after 15 minutes, reduced motion and axe; they were read from the code. Verification is Vitest (`viewer-machine.test.ts`, `tray.test.ts`, `wording.test.ts`, `events.test.ts`, `sync.test.ts`), typecheck, lint and build.
- Posting and your own Stories (#75):
  - **"Your Story"** is always first in the tray (`orderTray` adds it with no Stories when you have none). With none it is a link with a "+" to `/app/stories/new`, named "Post a Story" (`trayEntryLabel`); with some it opens them in the viewer like anyone's.
  - **`/app/stories/new`** (`app/app/stories/new/page.tsx`, `instant = false` like Settings; `NewStoryForm`): a Photo / Text choice (radios drawn as two pills). Photo: "Choose a photo", paste anywhere in the form (files with no text alongside, as in the composer) or drop on the dashed area, all through the composer's `chooseImage` checks (PNG/JPEG/WebP/GIF, not empty, 10 MB); a refusal is a `role=alert` under the button; the preview is a local object URL, given back when replaced or when the screen goes; an optional caption. Text: the box with a "n / 700" count (code points), a "Background" radio group of the six tones (`STORY_TONES`, drawn with `TONES` from `person-avatar.tsx`, never hard-coded colours) and a preview of the words on the chosen tone. It is a TanStack Form like every form: each mode's fields are mounted only while that mode is chosen, so only they are validated (`storyTextSchema`, `captionSchema` in `posting.ts`: the server's rules, outer spaces dropped, a caption of only spaces is no caption). Share runs one mutation: upload (`sdk.upload`), then post (`sdk.post`); the upload of the chosen photo is remembered, so Share pressed again after the post failed posts the same upload instead of uploading again (a 404 from the post, the upload no longer usable, forgets it; an upload answered without an id counts as a failed upload); a failure remembers which step failed (`ShareFailure`) and `storyFailureMessage` (pure, Vitest) words it in place: the cap (409) in the server's words, posting too often (429) and uploading too often with the wait, 413 and 400 like other images, 404 (the upload can no longer be used) asks for the photo again. Share without a photo says "Choose a photo to share." and focuses the button. On success the Story goes into the tray at once (`recordStoryEvent` with `story.posted`, which the server's own event repeats harmlessly) and the screen goes to `/app`.
  - **Your own Stories in the viewer** (`OwnStoryControls`): a bar at the bottom with "Seen by N" (`seenByText`; the tray's `viewCount`, which `story.viewed` raises live), "Add a Story" (to posting) and a ⋯ menu "Options for your Story" with "Delete Story", which asks first inside the menu ("Delete this Story? It goes for everyone at once.", Delete Story / Cancel; focus on Cancel, back to the item when cancelled), the way Block does. Deleting (the generated `deleteMutation`) puts `story.removed` into the tray at once, so the viewer moves on to the next Story or closes (`sync` in the state machine), and focus goes to the viewer's Close button (the focused "Delete Story" goes with the Story, and focus outside the dialog would stop its keys working); a refusal is worded under the menu. The ⋯ trigger is drawn in the scrim's ink (`DropdownMenu` gained `triggerClassName`), since the page's muted ink does not read on the dark bar in the light theme. "Seen by N" opens a panel over the Story (`viewersOptions`, asked for again whenever the count changes) listing each viewer with Avatar, name and time, newest first as the server sends it, "Nobody has seen it yet." when empty; its heading takes focus, Done or Escape closes it (Escape closes only the panel) and focus goes back to "Seen by"; the bar behind it is `inert` while it is open, so Tab cannot reach the menu under it; the list is asked for again when the count changes, not again on opening. While the panel or the menu is open the Story waits (the state machine's `cover`, set by a `Cover` element mounted only while they are there) and the viewer's own keys are left to them.
  - **Not exercised in a browser** (no Playwright): the posting screen (picking, pasting, dropping, the preview, the tones, the count, each failure, the return to the list), the "+" entry, "Seen by N" growing live, the viewers panel, the menu and its question with their focus moves, deleting from the viewer, and axe on all of it; they were read from the code. Verification is Vitest (`posting.test.ts`, `tray.test.ts`, `viewer-machine.test.ts`, `wording.test.ts`), typecheck, lint and build.
