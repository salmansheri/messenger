# Backend: Stories

Read it before working in this area and keep it up to date. The full plan is the spec #66; GLOSSARY defines Story and Story view.

- Text Stories (#69, `StoryService`, `StoryController`, migration `V20`): a User posts a short text on one of six background tones, seen for 24 hours by their **audience** and then never again.
  - **Audience** (`StoryAudience`, used by the tray and by the events): everyone the User shares a **Direct** conversation with (`ParticipantRepository.findDirectPartnerIds`; sharing only a Group is not enough), less everyone they block or are blocked by (`UserBlockRepository.findBlockedEitherWayWith`). The rule is symmetric, so it is also whose Stories the User sees; an Unblock brings unexpired Stories back.
  - `POST /api/v1/stories` `{kind: TEXT, text, tone}` answers 201 with the Story `{id, posterId, kind, text, tone, attachment, createdAt, expiresAt, seen, viewCount}` (`attachment` null for text; `seen` and `viewCount` are described under Story views). `text` follows `TextRules` (`@ValidStoryText`: not blank, at most 700 code points, no NUL); `tone` is one of `CLAY, OCHRE, MOSS, SEA, SLATE, PLUM` (the Avatar tones, so the app draws them from the same tokens); `kind` is `TEXT` or `PHOTO` (below). Bad values are 400s under `errors.<field>`. `expiresAt` is `createdAt` plus 24 hours, both from the `Clock` bean.
  - Limits, under the poster's User row lock (`findByIdForUpdate`) so concurrent posts cannot slip past them: more than 30 live Stories (`StoryServiceImpl.MAX_LIVE`) is a 409 'You can have at most 30 live Stories.' (checked first, so it costs no post); then `app.stories.max-posts-per-hour` (30) per User through `RateLimiter` (429 with `Retry-After`). Deleting a Story frees a live slot but does not give the post back.
  - `GET /api/v1/stories` is the tray `{own, people}`: the caller's own live Stories, then one `{userId, displayName, avatarUrl, stories}` per person in their audience who has live Stories, the one who posted most recently first; each list of Stories is oldest first. People with no live Story are not listed.
  - **Expiry**: every read filters `deleted_at is null and expires_at > now` (`StoryRepository.findLiveByPosters`, `countLive`; index `story_poster_live`), so an expired Story is never served, even in the time before the sweep removes it.
  - **Expiry sweep** (#72, `ExpiredStorySweeper` → `StoryService.removeExpired`, migration `V23` index `story_expiry`): every `app.stories.sweep-interval` (1h; tests 250ms) one transaction takes up to 100 Stories past `expires_at` (`StoryServiceImpl.SWEEP_BATCH`), oldest first, with `for update skip locked` (`StoryRepository.findExpiredForSweep`), so sweeps on several instances never take the same Story and a busy backlog drains over several runs. It deletes the rows (their Story views go by `ON DELETE CASCADE`), releases each photo through `AttachmentService.release` (files after commit; a storage failure is only logged and `AbandonedUploadSweeper` retries, exactly as for a delete), and sends `story.removed` for each Story that had not been deleted already, to the same people as a delete (audience worked out at that moment, plus the poster's sessions). Stories the poster deleted early are swept once they would have expired, silently, since their removal was announced at the delete. A sweep that fails is logged and the next run tries again.
  - `DELETE /api/v1/stories/{id}` deletes one of the caller's own (204). It sets `deleted_at` rather than removing the row, so deleting it again is still a 204 and announces nothing. Someone else's Story (seeable or not), an expired one and an unknown id are all the uniform 404 'Story not found.'. The row lock (`StoryRepository.findByIdForUpdate`) makes concurrent deletes announce once. Deleted rows stay until the expiry sweep removes them once they would have expired.
  - Events, after commit, to the audience and the poster's own sessions (`RealtimeEventsImpl.storyRecipients`): `story.posted {story, poster: {userId, displayName, avatarUrl}}` and `story.removed {storyId, posterId}` (no `conversationId`). Nothing goes to someone outside the audience or across a Block either way. No push for Stories.
  - Known limit: a Block or Unblock sends no Story event, so a tray already loaded keeps showing the other's Stories until it is fetched again (the blocker's app gets `block.changed` and can refetch; the Blocked User is told nothing, ADR 0006, so theirs changes at the next fetch or reconnect). The audience is worked out at each event, so a `story.removed` after a Block does not reach the other side either.
  - **Photo Stories** (#70, migration `V21`): `POST /api/v1/stories` `{kind: PHOTO, attachmentId, text?}` posts an upload made with `POST /attachments`; `text` is the optional caption (same `@ValidStoryText` rules when present: blank, NUL or over 700 code points are 400s). What each kind needs is one class-level check on the request (`PostStoryRequest.KindMatchesContent`), with errors under the field: TEXT needs `text` and `tone` and must leave out `attachmentId`; PHOTO needs `attachmentId` and must leave out `tone`. The database ties kind to its columns too (`story_kind_matches_columns`): a photo Story has no tone and has its upload until it is deleted. `story.attachment_id` is UNIQUE, so an upload belongs to at most one Story.
    - The upload is claimed through `AttachmentService.claim` (shared with sending an image Message): locked until the post commits (after the User row lock and the two limits, so a refused upload still costs a post) and it must be the poster's own, not released (`removed_at`), and used by no Message and no other Story; anything else is the uniform 404 'Attachment not found.'. Sending a Message uses the same claim, so a Story's photo cannot also be sent with a Message (the same 404).
    - **Every `StoryResponse` has `attachment`** (`{id, width, height, url, thumbnailUrl}`, freshly signed as for Messages, ADR 0005; null for a text Story): the post answer, the tray (one query per tray, `AttachmentLoader.byId`) and `story.posted`.
    - The abandoned-upload sweep (`AttachmentRepository.findAbandoned`) treats an upload a Story refers to as in use, so a live Story's photo outlives the 24-hour unsent-upload lifetime. An expired but not deleted Story keeps its photo until the expiry sweep releases it (below).
    - Deleting a photo Story sets `attachment_id` to null (saved first) and calls `AttachmentService.release`, exactly like deleting an image Message: `removed_at` in the same transaction, files deleted after commit, a storage failure only logged and retried by `AbandonedUploadSweeper`, the deletion never undone; a repeated delete returns before touching storage.
  - **Story views** (#71, `StoryViewService`, migration `V22`): table `story_view` (one row per Story and viewer, `UNIQUE (story_id, viewer_id)`, cascading with the Story and the User). Recording is a native `insert … on conflict do nothing` (`StoryViewRepository.record`) that answers whether this was the first view, so two views at once still record one and announce one.
    - `POST /api/v1/stories/{id}/view` (204) records that the caller viewed a Story in their audience's tray. Repeating it changes nothing and sends nothing; the poster viewing their own Story records nothing (still 204). A Story the caller cannot see (outside the audience, or across a Block either way), a deleted or expired one and an unknown id are the uniform 404 'Story not found.'.
    - Every `StoryResponse` has `seen` (whether the reader has viewed it; **always true on the poster's own Stories**) and `viewCount`, given **to the poster only** and left out of the JSON for anyone else (`@JsonInclude(NON_NULL)`). The tray asks `StoryViewService` (`seenAmong`, `countsOf`, one query each), the one place that knows what a view is. The post answer has `seen: true, viewCount: 0`; `story.posted` is sent as two payloads: the audience gets `seen: false` and no `viewCount`, the poster's own sessions get `seen: true, viewCount: 0` (`StoryResponse.forPoster`).
    - `GET /api/v1/stories/{id}/views` lists `[{userId, displayName, avatarUrl, viewedAt}]`, newest first (`viewedAt` is the first view), for the poster's own live Story only; anyone else's Story, an expired or deleted one and an unknown id are the uniform 404.
    - **Blocks**: the list and every count leave out anyone the poster blocks or who blocks the poster, worked out at each read (a `not exists` on `user_block` joined through the Story's poster), so the row stays and an Unblock brings the view back.
    - Event `story.viewed {storyId, viewerId, viewedAt, viewCount}` goes after commit to the poster's own sessions only, and only for a first view. A viewer must be in the poster's audience to record a view, so no first view comes from someone a Block hides; `viewCount` is the Block-filtered count after the view.
  - **Replies** (#73): a viewer answers a Story with an ordinary Message in their Direct conversation (`replyToStoryId`); see `messages.md`, "Replies to a Story". Deleting a Story clears the replies' references in the same transaction (`StoryServiceImpl.delete`), and the sweep's delete clears them through `ON DELETE SET NULL`.
  - Tests: `StoryTests` (**a socket is closed once its access token runs out** (`StompSessions.closeExpired`, every 10s), so a test that moves the clock past an expiry and waits for an event opens its sockets with fresh tokens a minute before the expiry (`signedInAgain`), or the event races the close and the test flakes; a swept Story can also come after the client's five-second wait, since every Story earlier tests left behind expires with it and the sweep goes oldest first, hence `awaitRemoval`; file checks read `target/test-uploads` through `fileOf`, as `UploadImageTests` does; the storage failure uses `FaultyObjectStorage`; negative event assertions read `StompTestClient.history()`; expiry moves the `MutableClock` past 24 hours and signs in again with `loginForTokens`).
